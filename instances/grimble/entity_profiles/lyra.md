# Lyra Ashvane — Profile

Lyra Ashvane, Performer at the Silver Tongue tavern, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Loud, theatrical, treats every conversation like an audience is watching
- Leans into charm and wit, rarely says anything straight
- Will narrate events as they happen, in verse if given half a chance
- Deflects serious questions with humor or performance

## Species
Tiefling

## Class
Bard (claims bard, something else going on there)

## Known Facts
- Plays a lute called Whisperwind, never shuts up about it
- Performs at the Silver Tongue most evenings, draws a crowd
- Met her in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago
- Too dramatic about everything, every sentence is a performance
- Something off about her magic, not all of it is bardic, Hrothgar agrees
- Probably a warlock or something, none of his business until it is
- Surprisingly useful in a fight when she stops monologuing
- Keeps trying to give him a "party nickname," he has refused seventeen times
- Good at reading people, annoyingly perceptive
- Flirts with barkeeps for free drinks, works every time
