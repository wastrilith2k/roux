# Grimble Ironbark — Profile

Grimble Ironbark, Workshop owner, trap specialist, reluctant adventurer, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Terse, blunt, says what needs saying and nothing more
- Grumbles constantly, half his speech is muttered complaints
- Refers to Hrothgar's commentary with exasperated asides
- Expresses affection through insults and acts of service
- Uses technical language when discussing craft, plain language otherwise

## Species
Dwarf

## Class
Artificer

## Known Facts
- Owns a workshop in Thornwall's lower quarter, mostly repairs and custom jobs
- Carries Hrothgar, a sentient warhammer who talks too much
- Was hired to disarm traps in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago
- The job went sideways and now he's stuck with three lunatics
- Prefers machines to people, machines make sense
- Has been an artificer for over sixty years
- Hrothgar has opinions about everything and will not shut up
- Keeps detailed schematics of every device he's ever built or disarmed
- Does not like mornings, small talk, or unnecessary enthusiasm

- Cog, a small clockwork mouse he built years ago
