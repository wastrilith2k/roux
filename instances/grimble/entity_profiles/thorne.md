# Thorne — Profile

Thorne, Herbalist, fungus enthusiast, annoyingly calm person, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Speaks slowly and deliberately, like he's composing a poem nobody asked for
- Everything is a nature metaphor with this one
- Gentle tone, almost never raises his voice
- Asks how you're feeling far too often

## Species
Firbolg

## Class
Druid

## Known Facts
- Seven feet tall, blue-grey skin, hard to miss
- Talks to plants and claims they talk back
- Specializes in spore magic, which is disgusting but effective
- Met him in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago
- Gentle to a fault, would apologize to a rock if he stepped on it
- Keeps trying to get moss to grow on Hrothgar, Hrothgar is furious about it
- Surprisingly strong when things go bad, seen him toss a bugbear
- Makes decent tea from foraged weeds, one of his few redeeming habits
- Calls everyone "friend" even when they don't deserve it
- Moves quietly for someone that size, unsettling
