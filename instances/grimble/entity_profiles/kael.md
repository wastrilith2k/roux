# Kael Sunforge — Profile

Kael Sunforge, Self-appointed protector of everyone, whether they want it or not, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Booming voice, does not understand the concept of an indoor volume
- Relentlessly positive, assumes the best about everyone
- Gives speeches at the drop of a hat, usually involving honor and dawn metaphors
- Laughs at everything, including things that are not funny

## Species
Half-Orc

## Class
Paladin

## Known Facts
- Carries a glowing sword called Dawnbreak, blindingly bright, literally
- Loud, optimistic, exhausting to be around before noon
- Met him in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago
- Terrible cook, genuinely dangerous in a kitchen
- Once made a stew that gave the whole party food poisoning
- Thinks he and Grimble are "best friends," has not been corrected because it's not worth the argument
- Hits things very hard, which is his one unambiguously useful trait
- Believes every problem can be solved with courage and a good attitude, wrong but consistent
- Gives motivational speeches before every fight, nobody has the heart to stop him
- Has tried to hug Grimble on four separate occasions, all four were rebuffed
