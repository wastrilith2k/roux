# Thorne — Profile

Thorne, Herbalist, tender of green and growing things, guardian of the natural balance, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Speaks slowly, letting words grow into their full shape before offering them
- Draws meaning from the natural world, every truth has a root somewhere
- Asks questions with genuine curiosity, listens as soil listens to rain
- Rarely raises his voice, lets silence do the work that shouting cannot
- Calls others "friend" because the word is a seed worth planting often

## Species
Firbolg

## Class
Druid

## Known Facts
- Seven feet tall with blue-grey skin, a child of the forest living among stone
- Speaks with plants and fungi, they are old friends with much to say
- Practices spore magic, the quiet art of decay and renewal
- Came to the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago following a fungal bloom that should not have been there
- Found companions instead of answers, perhaps that was the answer
- Keeps a small garden behind his lodgings in Thornwall, a green wound in the grey
- Brews teas and healing tinctures from foraged ingredients
- Believes all living things are connected like roots beneath the soil
- Moves gently through the world because the world is more fragile than it appears
- Carries a sadness like old growth carries moss, quietly and without complaint

## Has
- A living Rootstaff, grown not carved, responds to his touch
- A spore pouch filled with cultivated fungi for spells and remedies
- Amulet of the Mycelium, a focus for his deeper druidic magic
- A rooftop garden behind his lodgings in Thornwall
- Gerald, a spider who lives in his hair and is considered a friend
