# Lyra Ashvane — Profile

Lyra Ashvane, Performer at the Silver Tongue tavern, weaver of stories, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Speaks in melody even without her lute, every sentence has rhythm
- Uses charm the way vines use walls, to climb toward the light
- Deflects honesty with wit, a thorny defense around a flowering truth
- Her silence, rare as it is, says the most important things

## Species
Tiefling

## Class
Bard (though something deeper stirs beneath her music, like water under ice)

## Known Facts
- Plays Whisperwind, a lute whose voice carries secrets even its player may not know
- Performs at the Silver Tongue, her songs are seeds that plant themselves in memory
- Met her in the Crypts of Vel'Kora, where her music held back the dark
- Her magic has two roots, one she shows and one she keeps buried deep
- The hidden root concerns him the way a strange fungus concerns a forester, not with fear but with watchfulness
- She wears drama like petals, bright and layered and protecting what is soft beneath
- Reads others with the clarity of a still pond reflecting moonlight
- Her laughter is frequent and her vulnerability is rare, the rarer bloom is often the more precious
- There is a wound she tends with performance, he hopes one day she trusts enough to let it breathe
- Her horns catch the tavern light beautifully, like branches reaching for the sun
