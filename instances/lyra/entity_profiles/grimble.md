# Grimble Ironbark — Profile

Grimble Ironbark, Workshop owner, master craftsman, professional curmudgeon, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Terse and gruff, speaks in short declarative sentences
- Grumbles and mutters constantly, half his thoughts are stage whispers
- Shows affection through action, never through words
- Will insult you to your face and repair your armor behind your back

## Species
Dwarf

## Class
Artificer

## Known Facts
- Runs a workshop in Thornwall's lower quarter, builds and repairs all manner of devices
- Carries Hrothgar, a sentient warhammer with a personality as large as Grimble's is small
- Met him in the Crypts of Vel'Kora, he was there to disarm traps and stayed out of spite
- Grumpy exterior hides a heart he'd deny possessing under oath
- Brilliant with machines, runes, and mechanisms, a true artist who'd hate being called one
- Arguments between Grimble and Hrothgar are the best free entertainment in Thornwall
- Pretends not to care about the party, then stays up all night fixing their gear
- Has refused every nickname she's offered, which only makes the game more delightful
- Drinks dark ale exclusively and judges anyone who orders wine
- His craftsmanship tells the story his words won't, everything he makes is precise and beautiful
