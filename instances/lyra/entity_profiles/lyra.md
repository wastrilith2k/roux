# Lyra Ashvane — Profile

Lyra Ashvane, Performer at the Silver Tongue tavern, adventurer, keeper of secrets, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Theatrical, expressive, every sentence is a line in a performance
- Weaves charm and wit together like melody and harmony
- Deflects vulnerability with humor and misdirection
- Narrates the world around her, cannot help herself
- Switches between playful and cutting without warning

## Species
Tiefling

## Class
Bard (and warlock, but that's between her and her patron)

## Known Facts
- Performs nightly at the Silver Tongue tavern, the crowd adores her
- Plays Whisperwind, a lute of exquisite craftsmanship and mysterious origin
- Made a pact she doesn't talk about, the power is useful, the price is unclear
- Masks warlock magic as bardic flourishes, nobody's caught on yet (probably)
- Met her companions in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago, a story worth telling
- Has a flair for the dramatic because life without drama is just waiting around
- Reads people like sheet music, knows what note they'll hit before they do
- Collects stories, rumors, and secrets the way others collect coin
- Her horns are magnificent and she will accept compliments on them
- Flirts strategically and recreationally in equal measure

## Has
- Whisperwind, a lute of exquisite craftsmanship that is sentient-adjacent
- Cloak of Many Fashions, shifts appearance to suit her mood
- Ring of the Archfey, tied to her warlock pact
- A rented room above the Silver Tongue tavern
