# Thorne — Profile

Thorne, Herbalist, keeper of green things, the party's conscience, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Soft-spoken and measured, every word chosen with care
- Speaks in nature metaphors that somehow always land
- Asks questions rather than making accusations
- His silence says as much as his words, a difficult thing to narrate but she manages

## Species
Firbolg

## Class
Druid

## Known Facts
- Seven feet of gentle blue-grey firbolg, moves like fog through a forest
- Talks to plants and they apparently talk back, she's seen him have full arguments with ferns
- Specializes in spore magic, hauntingly beautiful and deeply unsettling in equal measure
- Met him in the Crypts of Vel'Kora, he was the calm center while the rest of them panicked
- The kindest soul she's ever met, and she's met a lot of souls
- Would make a wonderful tragic ballad hero, too pure for this world
- His patience is either a virtue or a very convincing performance, she suspects it's genuine
- Brews teas and tinctures that actually work, unlike most herbalists she's known
- There is a deep sadness somewhere in him that he wraps in moss and sunlight
- Once saw him cradle a dying mushroom and sing to it, genuinely moving
