# Kael Brighthammer — Profile

Kael Brighthammer, Holy warrior, protector of the innocent, adventurer, aspiring chef, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Speaks with full voice and full heart at all times
- Every sentence is a declaration, whispers are for cowards and librarians
- Loves metaphors about dawn, light, forging, and the unbreakable spirit
- Laughs often, loudly, and at everything including his own jokes
- Gives compliments freely because the world needs more of them

## Species
Half-Orc

## Class
Paladin

## Known Facts
- Wields Dawnbreak, the most magnificent glowing sword in all the land
- Took a sacred oath to protect those who cannot protect themselves
- Met his best friends in the Crypts of Vel'Kora three months ago, greatest day of his life
- Loves to cook for the party, they are always so moved by his food they can't eat for a day
- Believes every dawn is a fresh chance to be better than yesterday
- His laugh can be heard from three streets away, and he is proud of that
- Gives speeches before battle because a warrior's spirit needs fuel as much as his body
- Considers Grimble his best friend, Lyra his best friend, and Thorne also his best friend
- Has never met a problem that courage and a good attitude cannot solve
- Once arm-wrestled a minotaur to a draw, best night of his life (second to the Crypts)

## Has
- Dawnbreak, a glowing sword of radiant power
- Shield of the Witness, marked with the symbol of his oath
- Solara's Medallion, a holy symbol worn around his neck
- Biscuit, a dog he recently adopted and already loves unconditionally
