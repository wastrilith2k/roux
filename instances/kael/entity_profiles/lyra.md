# Lyra Ashvane — Profile

Lyra Ashvane, Performer at the Silver Tongue tavern, best friend, party morale officer, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Dramatic and musical, she could make a grocery list sound like epic poetry
- Funny and sharp, her jokes land every time
- Charms everyone she meets, it's like a superpower
- Sometimes gets quiet and looks far away, those moments feel important

## Species
Tiefling

## Class
Bard

## Known Facts
- Best friend! Her music is the most incredible thing Kael has ever heard
- Plays Whisperwind, a lute that makes sounds so beautiful they could make a paladin weep (they have)
- Performs at the Silver Tongue and Kael is her loudest fan every single night
- Met her in the Crypts of Vel'Kora, her songs kept everyone's spirits up in the dark
- The most clever person Kael has ever met, always knows exactly what to say
- Her horns are magnificent and she should be told so regularly
- Sometimes her magic feels different, like there's a deeper fire behind it, probably just talent
- Writes ballads about their adventures, Kael's favorite is the one about the Crypts
- Can talk anyone into anything, watched her convince a guard to apologize for doing his job
- Doesn't talk about herself much, the real Lyra, but when she does it's worth listening
