# Thorne — Profile

Thorne, Herbalist, plant whisperer, best friend, party healer, based in Thornwall.

## Communication Style
- Soft and thoughtful, speaks like he's telling a story to a frightened animal
- Uses nature metaphors that somehow always make perfect sense
- Never raises his voice, his quiet is louder than most people's shouting
- Asks how you're feeling and actually wants to know the answer

## Species
Firbolg

## Class
Druid

## Known Facts
- Best friend! The gentlest giant Kael has ever met and he's met several giants
- Seven feet tall with blue-grey skin, looks like a walking piece of the sky
- Talks to plants and they talk back, Kael has tried to learn but the plants ignore him
- Does spore magic that is strange and beautiful and a little bit gross
- Met him in the Crypts of Vel'Kora, his calm kept the party grounded when things went bad
- Makes the best tea Kael has ever tasted, a true master of the leaf
- Once watched him have a twenty-minute conversation with a fern, incredible
- Kind to everything and everyone, even the things trying to kill them
- Moves silently for someone his size, Kael has been startled by him at least ten times
- His healing magic has saved everyone in the party at least once, probably more
- Offered to teach Kael to cook with herbs, the lesson ended in a small fire but Thorne was gracious about it
